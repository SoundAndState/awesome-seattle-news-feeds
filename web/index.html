About this reader

Sound & State

What is this and why?

A browser-based RSS and Atom reader, made for fun and mainly used to preview content from Awesome Seattle News Feeds. It is not for profit. The maintainer collects no user data.

Storage and refresh

Reading state, preferences, and cached articles are stored locally in IndexedDB, with no account or server sync. Clearing browser data removes them. Backups are local JSON files; saved articles can also be downloaded as CSV.

“Mark articles read as I scroll past them” is off by default. When enabled, an article is marked read after you scroll it above the top of the page.

Healthy feeds refresh at most every 15 minutes. Failed feeds retry less often automatically; Refresh retries them immediately. Unsaved content is kept for 30 days, up to 150 items per feed. Saved items remain until removed.

Feed requests go to Cloudflare first. Successful responses are shared across readers for up to 15 minutes, or less when the publisher requires it. Errors and private responses are not cached. For KING 5, KREM 2, and MyNorthwest, a GitHub job collects fallback copies every 15 minutes (the schedule can be delayed). Cloudflare uses these only if its publisher request fails. Source details show when a fallback was collected; copies expire within 6 hours, sooner if the publisher requires revalidation.

Privacy and security

  • GitHub serves the site; Cloudflare proxies feed requests. Both receive your IP address and request metadata. Scheduled fallback fetches run on GitHub and send no visitor data to publishers. If the proxy and its fallback fail, your browser retries the feed directly. That publisher then receives your IP address and request metadata. Feed requests send no cookies, credentials, or referrer. Direct retries work only when the publisher permits cross-site access (CORS). Reading state is not uploaded. Searches and reader navigation stay in the URL fragment (after #), which is not sent to the site host.
  • No analytics or third-party fonts. Feed HTML is sanitized with DOMPurify; scripts, frames, forms, and remote images are removed. A content security policy restricts scripts and connections.
  • Article pages and archive searches open only when you follow their links. Those sites receive your IP address and may use cookies; links send no referrer. Ghostarchive searches receive the article URL with utm_* parameters removed. Archived copies may not exist.
