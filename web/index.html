About this reader

Sound & State

What is this and why?

A browser-based reader made for fun, mainly to preview Awesome Seattle News Feeds. It is not for profit. The maintainer collects no user data.

Privacy at a glance

  • No reader cookies or analytics. The reader does not set or read cookies. It uses no third-party fonts.
  • Your library stays here. Saved items, reading state, and preferences use IndexedDB in this browser. There is no account or server sync. Clearing browser data removes them.
  • Requests still reach servers. GitHub and Cloudflare receive your IP address and request metadata. Direct feed retries also disclose these to the publisher. Feed requests omit cookies, credentials, and referrers.
  • External sites have their own policies. Publisher and archive pages open when you follow their links and may use their own cookies.
Connections and content security

GitHub serves the site; Cloudflare proxies feeds. If its publisher request and any cached fallback fail, this browser retries the feed directly when the publisher permits cross-site access (CORS). Publisher cookies from feed requests are not stored in your browser. Reading state is not uploaded. Searches and navigation use the URL fragment (after #), which is not sent to the site host.

Feed HTML is sanitized with DOMPurify. Scripts, frames, forms, and remote images are removed. A content security policy restricts scripts and connections. Publisher and archive links send no referrer. Ghostarchive receives the article URL with utm_* parameters removed; an archived copy may not exist.

Storage and refresh

The active reading mode checks due feeds while visible and when you return to the tab. Healthy feeds are checked at most every 15 minutes; failed feeds back off. Refresh retries failures immediately. New arrivals wait behind a button while you read. Unsaved content is retained for 30 days, up to 150 items per feed. Saved items remain until removed.

Cloudflare shares successful responses for up to 15 minutes, or less when required by the publisher. Errors and private responses are not cached. A GitHub job collects fallback copies for KING 5, KREM 2, and MyNorthwest every 15 minutes, subject to scheduling delays. These expire within 6 hours, sooner when required. Source details show collection times. Scheduled fetches send no visitor data to publishers.

The last validated feed catalog is retained for recovery during connection failures. This is not a fully offline application. Automatic scroll marking is off by default and marks an item after you scroll it above the page.

Back up your library

Export read status and saved items to a local JSON file, or restore an existing backup. CSV export is available above the reading list.
