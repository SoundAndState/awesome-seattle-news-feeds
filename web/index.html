Stay close to the story.

Sound & State brings the Awesome Seattle News Feeds collection into one quiet reading space. Reporting, opinion, advocacy, official updates, and satire are grouped into labeled sections.

Your browser, your reading list

Read status, saved stories, and downloaded articles stay on this device. There are no accounts or analytics. Clearing browser data removes this history; use a backup to keep it.

Fresh stories, at your pace

Feeds refresh when you open the reader and when you choose Refresh, at most once every 15 minutes per feed. Only the selected section or source is refreshed when a filter is active. Unavailable feeds are retried less often; previously loaded stories stay visible. Unsaved articles are kept for 30 days, up to 150 per feed. Saved stories stay until you remove them.

Visit and support the source

This reader shows what publishers include in their RSS feeds. Some provide excerpts or require a subscription for the full story. “Read at publisher” opens the original article.

Feeds travel through this project’s Cloudflare relay so your browser can read them. GitHub and Cloudflare receive ordinary connection information; publishers receive requests from the relay. Article previews don’t load remote images or trackers.

For Seattle Times subscriptions and article archives, see the collection’s reading and archiving guidance. Software credits.
